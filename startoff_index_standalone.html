FAQ

Good to know.

Is the annual price fixed?

No. Ask STARTOFF for the current custom price and written plan terms before payment.

Does an enquiry mean my release is approved?

No. Submissions require review and must meet rights, metadata and distributor requirements. Delivery is not automatic or guaranteed.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions and the amount must be confirmed by STARTOFF. Any transfer is verified manually; keep your payment reference.

How do I send audio and artwork?

Start with a WhatsApp enquiry. The team will confirm file requirements and the appropriate way to share materials.

Is worldwide distribution available?

Worldwide enquiries are welcome. Actual platform and territory availability depends on the authorized distribution route confirmed by STARTOFF.

Are streams or royalties guaranteed?

No. Streaming performance varies, and royalty reporting depends on the distributor and platform data available for each release.
